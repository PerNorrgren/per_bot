Savers Protocol
Cancellation win-back: Day 0 fires the moment someone schedules a cancellation — an acknowledgment only, their paid time is untouched. Grace start/mid/final cover a bonus 14 days once their real term actually ends. Payment-failure win-back: Day 0 fires immediately on a genuine payment failure (not one already mid-cancellation), independent of Stripe's own retry schedule. Different voice on purpose between the two — this was a decision, not a mistake.
