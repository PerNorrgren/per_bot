Savers Protocol
Cancellation win-back
Day 0 fires the moment someone schedules a cancellation — an acknowledgment only, their paid time is untouched. Grace 0/Mid/Final cover a bonus 14 days once their real term actually ends. Different voice from the payment-failure side below on purpose: this was a decision, not a mistake.
Payment-failure win-back
Day 0 fires immediately on a genuine payment failure (not one already mid-cancellation). A firm 14-day window from that moment, independent of Stripe's own retry schedule. Warm and practical, not a sales pitch — usually nobody's fault at all.
