The Live Teaching Companion

Most of the time you're teaching alone. If someone asks a question you didn't expect, you either wing it, or the moment's gone. This is a way around that — a live conversation partner, most likely open on a second screen while you teach on Zoom, that knows the entire framework properly: every signal, the three big worries the nervous system carries, the Moro Reflex Brake, all of it.

Pick the session you're covering, and it already knows your own notes for it. Ask by typing or by voice. And choose how the answer comes back — Plain, the way you'd actually say it to a room; Clinical, professional and precise; or Technical, the full mechanistic answer. Same real science underneath, every time — only the language changes.
