My conversations with the companion
▾
Keep history between conversations
When this is on, the companion remembers a gentle thread from one self-guided conversation to the next — a short summary and sense of where you're at, so it doesn't start from zero each time. When it's off, every conversation starts completely fresh, and nothing about what you say is kept afterward.
This is separate from any work you do directly with a facilitator, which has its own confidentiality and isn't affected by this setting either way.
