When I move between giving and pulling back — Reflecting

When I move between giving and pulling back — CARE and SEEKING cycle between giving fully and needing distance. This sits within Complex 7 (Contribution & Caregiving), in its oscillating state. This stays close to inner experience, naming the push–pull around care without resolving it or turning it into something to manage.

There are moments when care feels available and natural. Offering help feels right. Attention moves outward without strain.

And then, just as clearly, there are moments when that openness recedes. Distance feels necessary. Pulling back brings relief.

The shift can be subtle. A change in energy. A sense of having reached a limit without noticing where it was.

This movement can be confusing. Especially for those who value being reliable, present, able to contribute.

The mind may look for consistency. Why can’t I just give steadily? Why does my availability keep changing?

Often, what’s moving is capacity rather than intention. Care rises when there is room. It withdraws when that room fills.

There may be guilt on one side of the shift. A sense of letting others down. And resentment on the other, when giving stretches too far.

Neither feeling is wrong. Both point to the same truth — that care has limits, even when the heart remains willing.

Recognising this doesn’t stabilise the rhythm. It doesn’t decide when to give or when to stop.

It simply allows the experience of moving between giving and pulling back to be named honestly, as part of how caregiving stays possible over time.
