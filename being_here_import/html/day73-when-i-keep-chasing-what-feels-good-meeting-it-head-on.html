When I keep chasing what feels good — Meeting It Head On

When I keep chasing what feels good — SEEKING, PLAY, and CARE intensify. This sits within Complex 9 (Joy–Integration & Exploration), in its over-activated state. This poem implicitly carries all nine somatic / salience steps in order, and works to let joy land and integrate without flattening aliveness or dampening curiosity. It allows pleasure to arrive without pursuit.

There is space here to stop reaching for the next moment. Nothing is slipping away right now.

The body is already supported, held without needing to move toward anything, steady without momentum.

Awareness settles into where you are, not scanning for novelty, not leaning ahead of itself.

Muscles release their forward pull, not losing energy, just letting it rest.

Breath moves easily again, no longer quickened by anticipation, no longer stretching toward what comes next.

Warmth gathers softly, not sparkling outward, not demanding more, content to stay.

The chest softens without flattening. Excitement doesn’t disappear — it quiets enough to be felt.

The urge to seek is allowed without being followed. Interest can pause without becoming boredom.

The body recognises that joy does not need to be chased to be real.

There is a sense of being here with what is pleasant now, without needing it to expand or continue.

This moment does not ask you to stop enjoying. It does not ask you to settle forever.

You remain here, inside a body that can feel pleasure and let it land, without running ahead.

For now, that is enough.
