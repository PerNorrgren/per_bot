When closeness feels distant — Reflecting

When closeness feels distant — CARE and LUST run muted. This sits within Complex 8 (Intimacy & Fusion), in its under-activated state. This stays close to inner experience, naming distance and reserve without urging openness, warmth, or connection.

There are times when connection feels far away, even in the presence of others. Not absent, just out of reach.

Closeness may appear theoretical — something that exists for other people, or in other moments, but not quite here.

This distance is often quiet. Not dramatic. More like a holding back that happens without conscious choice.

It can feel protective. Safer to stay contained. Easier not to risk being affected.

Sometimes there is a history behind this. Times when closeness arrived too fast. Or when letting someone in led to confusion or loss.

At other times, the distance feels unexplained. A flatness. A neutrality. A sense of watching from the edge.

The mind may judge this quickly. Why don’t I feel more? Why can’t I connect the way others seem to?

But distance is not indifference. It can be a form of pacing. A way of staying intact when openness feels uncertain.

Recognising this doesn’t make closeness appear. It doesn’t warm what feels cool.

It simply allows the experience of closeness feeling distant to be held with more understanding, as a state that protects as much as it limits.
