When I’m always measuring where I stand — Meeting It Head On

When I’m always measuring where I stand — RAGE, FEAR, and SEEKING run high together. This sits within Complex 6 (Status & Hierarchy), in its over-activated state. This poem implicitly carries all nine somatic / salience steps in order, and works to settle status vigilance without collapsing competence or ambition. It allows worth to stabilise independently of position.

There is space here to stop checking for a moment. Nothing is being assessed right now.

The body is already supported, held without needing to rise or fall, steady without climbing.

Awareness settles into where you are, not scanning above or below, not tracking who is ahead or behind.

Muscles release their quiet readiness, not losing strength, just easing the constant comparison.

Breath moves freely again, no longer shaped by anticipation, no longer preparing for judgement.

Warmth stays close and contained, not surging to impress, not withdrawing to hide.

The chest remains upright without strain, not puffed up, not shrinking back, able to hold itself.

The sense of value gathers inward, less dependent on reflection, less tied to how you appear.

The body recognises that position can change without threatening existence.

There is a feeling of standing on your own ground, not ranked, not evaluated, simply present.

This moment does not ask you to improve your place. It does not ask you to lower yourself.

You remain here, inside a body that knows its worth without needing to compare, without needing to prove.

For now, that is enough.
