When I move between merging and pulling away — Meeting It Head On

When I move between merging and pulling away — LUST, CARE, and PLAY cycle between merging and pulling away. This sits within Complex 8 (Intimacy & Fusion), in its oscillating state. This poem implicitly carries all nine somatic / salience steps in order, and works with shifting intimacy without forcing balance, commitment, or withdrawal. It allows closeness and separateness to coexist.

There is space here for closeness and for distance. Nothing needs to settle into one way of being.

The body is already supported, able to lean in and to return to itself without losing its shape.

Awareness widens to include the movement — the draw toward connection, the need for space, both arriving honestly.

Muscles adjust naturally, not melting forward, not pulling back sharply, responding to what feels possible.

Breath continues freely, moving through approach and through pause, without choosing sides.

Warmth shifts gently, sometimes reaching outward, sometimes staying close, remaining present throughout.

The chest does not have to decide how open to be. It can stay responsive, meeting each moment as it comes.

The wish to merge is allowed without being followed completely. The wish to withdraw is allowed without becoming absence.

The body learns that intimacy does not require permanence, and distance does not mean disconnection.

There is a sense of staying with yourself through nearness and space, a steady presence beneath the change.

This moment does not ask you to choose closeness or to pull away.

You remain here, inside a body that can move toward and back, touch and pause, without losing itself.

For now, that is enough.
