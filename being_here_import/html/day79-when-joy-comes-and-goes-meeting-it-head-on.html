When joy comes and goes — Meeting It Head On

When joy comes and goes — SEEKING, PLAY, and CARE cycle, arriving and then fading. This sits within Complex 9 (Joy–Integration & Exploration), in its oscillating state. This poem implicitly carries all nine somatic / salience steps in order, and works with the coming and going of joy without trying to stabilise it, intensify it, or hold onto it. It allows pleasure to move naturally without being chased or lost.

There is space here for enjoyment and for quiet. Nothing needs to stay.

The body is already supported, able to receive what arrives and to remain when it leaves, without shifting its ground.

Awareness widens to include the change — the moment of lightness, the moment of neutrality, both allowed.

Muscles soften without leaning forward, and without collapsing back, resting in between.

Breath continues on its own, moving through pleasure and through its absence, unchanged.

Warmth shifts gently, sometimes more noticeable, sometimes barely there, remaining present throughout.

The chest does not have to hold joy in place. It does not have to protect against its fading.

Enjoyment is allowed to appear without being secured. Flatness is allowed without being feared.

The body recognises that joy is not a possession. It is a visitor that comes and goes.

There is a sense of staying here through brightness and quiet, inside the same steady presence.

This moment does not ask you to keep joy alive or to explain its absence.

You remain here, inside a body that can feel pleasure and let it pass, without losing itself.

For now, that is enough.
