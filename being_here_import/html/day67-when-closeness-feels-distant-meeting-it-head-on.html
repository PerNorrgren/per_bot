When closeness feels distant — Meeting It Head On

When closeness feels distant — CARE and LUST run muted. This sits within Complex 8 (Intimacy & Fusion), in its under-activated state. This poem implicitly carries all nine somatic / salience steps in order, and works to allow closeness to approach gently without demand, pressure, or loss of autonomy. It supports contact without intrusion.

There is no requirement here to move nearer. Nothing is asking you to open.

The body is already supported, held by its own structure, allowed to remain as it is.

Awareness settles where you are, not reaching outward, not guarding against approach, just staying present.

Muscles soften without inviting touch, not bracing, not withdrawing, simply resting.

Breath moves quietly, uninvolved, neither shared nor withheld.

Warmth stays close, subtle and contained, enough to be felt without spreading.

The chest remains steady. It does not need to widen. It does not need to close.

Distance is allowed here. So is neutrality. Neither is a problem.

The body recognises that closeness does not have to arrive suddenly. It can approach slowly, or not at all.

There is a sense of being here without needing to connect, whole without engagement.

This moment does not ask you to soften faster or to let anything in.

You remain here, inside a body that can stay intact while allowing the possibility of closeness to exist at the edges.

For now, that is enough.
