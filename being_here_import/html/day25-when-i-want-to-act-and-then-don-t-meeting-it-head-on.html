When I want to act and then don’t — Meeting It Head On

When I want to act and then don’t — SEEKING and RAGE cycle between surging and dropping away. This sits within Complex 3 (Agency & Reward), in its oscillating state. This poem implicitly carries all nine somatic / salience steps in order and works with fluctuating agency without trying to stabilise it into consistency or push it toward productivity. It allows movement and rest to coexist.

There is room here for movement and pause.

Nothing needs to settle into one pace.

The body is already supported,

able to lean forward and to rest back without losing its place.

Awareness widens to include change —

the rise of intention,

the softening of interest,

both arriving honestly.

Energy gathers for a moment,

then releases.

Neither is treated as failure.

Breath continues without aligning itself with starting or stopping,

moving through both without preference.

Warmth shifts subtly,

sometimes brighter,

sometimes quieter,

remaining available throughout.

The chest doesn’t have to commit.

It can stay responsive,

meeting each phase as it comes.

The urge to act is allowed to appear without being forced into completion.

The slowing is allowed without being explained away.

The body learns that fluctuation is not collapse.

Changing direction does not mean losing agency.

There is a sense of staying present through momentum and through pause,

inside the same steady outline.

This moment does not ask for consistency.

It does not ask you to decide who you are in motion.

You remain here,

inside a body that can begin and stop,

try and rest,

without breaking trust with itself.

For now, that is enough.
