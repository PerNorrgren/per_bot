When I want closeness and then don’t — Meeting It Head On

When I want closeness and then don’t — PANIC/GRIEF and CARE swing between reaching and pulling back. This sits within Complex 2 (Attachment & Belonging), in its oscillating state. This poem implicitly carries all nine somatic / salience steps in order, and works with the push–pull rhythm of attachment without forcing resolution, choice, or coherence. It allows movement without instability, and contact without collapse.

There is space here for movement in both directions.

Nothing needs to settle into one position.

The body is already present,

supported where it is,

able to shift without losing its place.

Attention widens enough to include more than one feeling at a time —

the pull toward, and the pull away.

Neither needs to win.

The rhythm of this moment is allowed.

Approach and pause.

Interest and retreat.

Each arriving in its own time.

Breath continues without choosing sides,

moving in,

moving out,

not aligning itself with either urge.

Warmth comes and goes gently,

not flooding,

not disappearing,

staying available without insisting.

The chest doesn’t have to open fully or close itself down.

It can remain responsive,

adjusting moment by moment.

Wanting is felt without being acted on.

Distance is felt without being defended.

The body learns that movement itself does not mean danger.

Shifting does not mean losing contact.

There is a sense of staying with yourself even as feelings change direction,

a steady presence beneath the motion.

This moment does not require a decision.

It does not ask you to choose closeness or space.

You remain here,

inside a body that can move and pause,

reach and rest,

without falling apart.

For now,

that is enough.
