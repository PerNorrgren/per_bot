When I need connection right now — Meeting It Head On

When I need connection right now — PANIC/GRIEF and CARE run high. This sits within Complex 2 (Attachment & Belonging), in its over-activated state. This poem implicitly carries all nine somatic / salience steps in order, regulates PANIC/GRIEF + CARE, and works through receptive listening, not effort. It is wide, relational, and steady — easing urgency without withdrawing warmth.

There is space here for wanting.

Nothing about this moment

says you are too much.

The body is already somewhere solid,

supported without being held tightly,

resting without slipping away.

Attention settles into where you are,

not scanning outward,

not searching ahead,

just noticing this place has edges.

Movement slows naturally,

not because it’s asked to,

but because there is nothing pulling it forward.

Breath continues on its own,

arriving and leaving,

not trying to reach anyone,

not needing an answer back.

Warmth stays close to the centre,

steady rather than spreading,

present without leaning.

The chest softens without opening wide.

The hands don’t have to reach.

The body doesn’t have to follow the urge.

The feeling of wanting is allowed to exist,

without being fed and without being pushed away.

Connection doesn’t disappear here.

It simply stops rushing.

There is a sense of being accompanied

without anyone else entering the scene,

a quiet knowing that you

are not alone inside yourself.

This moment does not depend on response.

It does not require closeness to arrive.

You remain here,

held by your own presence,

able to wait without losing contact.

For now, that is enough.
