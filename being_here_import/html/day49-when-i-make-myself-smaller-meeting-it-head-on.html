When I make myself smaller — Meeting It Head On

When I make myself smaller — FEAR sits heavy while RAGE collapses. This sits within Complex 6 (Status & Hierarchy), in its under-activated state. This poem implicitly carries all nine somatic / salience steps in order, and works to restore vertical presence and dignity without pushing toward dominance, confidence, or exposure. It supports taking up space quietly.

There is space here to be the size you are. Nothing needs to be reduced.

The body is already supported, held by what’s beneath it, allowed to rise without lifting itself.

Awareness settles inside your outline, not shrinking back, not pressing forward, just noticing your place.

Muscles gather gently, not to defend, not to retreat, simply to hold you upright.

Breath moves easily again, not hidden, not restrained, moving through your whole shape.

Warmth stays present, not dimmed, not withdrawn, steady and contained.

The chest finds its own height, not puffed up, not folded inward, able to exist without apology.

The sense of being seen does not require performance. Visibility does not have to be earned.

The body recognises that taking up space does not threaten anyone.

There is a feeling of being here, noticeable without effort, solid without display.

This moment does not ask you to stand out or step aside.

You remain here, inside a body that is allowed to occupy its place without shrinking.

For now, that is enough.
