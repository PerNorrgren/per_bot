When joy feels far away — Meeting It Head On

When joy feels far away — SEEKING runs low and PLAY goes quiet. This sits within Complex 9 (Joy–Integration & Exploration), in its under-activated state. This poem implicitly carries all nine somatic / salience steps in order, and works to allow enjoyment to re-enter quietly without expectation, effort, or demand to feel good. It supports joy as something that arrives, not something to be generated.

There is no need here to feel anything special. Nothing is missing because enjoyment isn’t present.

The body is already supported, held without needing to respond, allowed to remain neutral.

Awareness settles into this moment, not searching for interest, not asking for colour or spark.

Weight rests where it falls, steady and ordinary, without pressure to lift.

Breath moves quietly, uninvolved, arriving and leaving without carrying expectation.

Warmth is subtle, barely noticeable, enough to be sensed without asking for more.

The chest stays as it is. It doesn’t need to open. It doesn’t need to close.

Flatness is allowed here. So is dullness. Neither needs fixing.

The body recognises that joy does not disappear forever when it goes quiet. It rests until there is room.

There is a sense of being here without needing to enjoy, present without disappointment.

This moment does not ask you to try harder or to brighten up.

You remain here, inside a body that can wait for pleasure to return in its own time.

For now, that is enough.
