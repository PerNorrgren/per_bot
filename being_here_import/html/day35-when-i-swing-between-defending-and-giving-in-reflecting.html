When I swing between defending and giving in — Reflecting

When I swing between defending and giving in — RAGE and FEAR cycle between defending hard and giving way. This sits within Complex 4 (Boundary & Fairness), in its oscillating state. This stays close to inner experience, naming fluctuation around boundaries without resolving it or turning it into a problem to fix.

There are moments when holding your ground feels essential.

And other moments when it feels easier to step aside.

The shift between these positions can be quick.

One situation invites firmness.

The next calls for accommodation.

From the inside, this movement can feel confusing.

Am I being too rigid? Too yielding?

Why can’t I find a middle place and stay there?

Often, both responses carry purpose.

Defending arises when something feels threatened.

Giving in appears when connection, peace, or safety feels more important.

Neither comes from nowhere.

Both are shaped by experience, by past consequences,

by what has felt possible in similar moments before.

The mind may search for consistency.

A stable rule to follow. A clear identity to inhabit.

But lived boundaries are rarely static.

They respond to context, to power, to timing,

to how much support is present.

Swinging between protecting and yielding doesn’t mean you lack clarity.

It often means you are navigating competing needs in real time.

Recognising this doesn’t resolve the movement. It doesn’t fix the oscillation.

It simply allows the experience to be seen with more honesty,

without turning it into failure or demanding it settle into one side.
