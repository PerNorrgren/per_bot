When nothing feels worth the effort — Meeting It Head On

When nothing feels worth the effort — SEEKING runs low and CARE goes muted. This sits within Complex 5 (Effort–Value), in its under-activated state. This poem implicitly carries all nine somatic / salience steps in order, and works with low valuation without motivating, persuading, or asking for engagement. It allows value to be felt again before effort is required.

There is no need here to make anything matter.

Nothing is being asked of you.

The body is already present,

supported without needing to invest,

allowed to remain where it is.

Awareness settles into this moment,

not searching for purpose,

not measuring importance.

Weight rests where it falls,

not heavy,

not light,

simply there.

Breath continues quietly,

unconcerned with meaning,

arriving and leaving without demand.

Warmth appears faintly,

subtle rather than motivating,

enough to register without needing to grow.

The chest stays neutral,

neither lifted by interest

nor collapsed by disengagement.

Indifference is allowed here.

So is weariness.

Neither needs to be corrected.

The body recognises that value

does not always announce itself as desire or urgency.

Sometimes worth is felt first as simple presence —

being here without wanting more.

This moment does not ask you to care harder.

It does not ask you to try again.

You remain here,

inside a body that can rest until something feels worth meeting,

in its own time.

For now, that is enough.
