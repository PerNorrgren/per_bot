When I lose myself in closeness — Meeting It Head On

When I lose myself in closeness — LUST, CARE, and PLAY intensify. This sits within Complex 8 (Intimacy & Fusion), in its over-activated state. This poem implicitly carries all nine somatic / salience steps in order, and works to restore differentiation without withdrawing intimacy. It allows closeness with edges.

There is space here to be close without disappearing.

The body is already supported, held by its own shape, able to remain distinct.

Awareness settles inside your outline, not drifting toward another, not dissolving outward, just noticing where you end.

Muscles gather softly, not pulling away, not melting forward, holding form without tension.

Breath moves freely, not synchronising, not reaching, staying your own.

Warmth remains present, intimate without flooding, close without overtaking.

The chest stays open enough to feel connection, and firm enough to stay intact.

The pull toward merging is allowed without being followed. Desire can exist without needing to erase distance.

The body recognises that intimacy does not require fusion. Closeness does not depend on losing yourself.

There is a sense of being here with another while still being you, clear and whole.

This moment does not ask you to step back or to move closer.

You remain here, inside a body that can touch and stay, feel and remain separate, without dissolving.

For now, that is enough.
