When I lose myself in closeness — Noticing

When I lose myself in closeness — LUST, CARE, and PLAY intensify. This sits within Complex 8 (Intimacy & Fusion), in its over-activated state. This places merging and loss of differentiation in a wider relational and cultural context, without turning inward, regulating, or resolving.

Many stories about intimacy emphasise union. Being “on the same wavelength.” Feeling inseparable. Wanting the same things at the same time.

In these narratives, closeness is often equated with sameness. Difference can feel like distance. Edges can be mistaken for disconnection.

Some environments actively encourage this. Romantic ideals prize fusion. Care is shown through attunement. Responsiveness is rewarded.

Within these conditions, softening boundaries can feel like success. Losing yourself can look like devotion. Merging can feel like safety.

For those who have learned that connection is fragile, staying closely aligned may reduce the risk of loss. Being separate might feel like stepping away.

Seen this way, dissolving into closeness is not accidental. It is shaped by cultural messages and relational experiences that associate intimacy with togetherness.

Noticing this doesn’t restore boundaries. It doesn’t create distance.

It simply places the experience within the frameworks that shape it, where losing yourself in closeness can feel like the most natural way to stay connected.
