When I move between merging and pulling away — Noticing

When I move between merging and pulling away — LUST, CARE, and PLAY cycle between merging and pulling away. This sits within Complex 8 (Intimacy & Fusion), in its oscillating state. This places the ebb and flow of intimacy in a wider relational and cultural context, without turning inward, regulating, or resolving.

Intimacy is shaped by more than feeling alone. It unfolds within timing, expectation, and how closeness has been held in the past.

Many cultures send mixed signals about intimacy. Togetherness is idealised. Independence is prized. Boundaries are praised in theory and tested in practice.

Relationships often move quickly. Depth is encouraged early. Space can be misunderstood as disinterest rather than pacing.

Within these conditions, moving toward and away from closeness becomes a way of negotiating safety. Approach tests connection. Withdrawal restores orientation.

This rhythm is not unusual. It reflects the need to balance belonging with autonomy, contact with selfhood.

Some environments allow this movement. Others pressure consistency — to stay open, to stay available, to stay the same.

When there is little room to adjust, the body does the adjusting itself, moving between closeness and distance to protect both connection and self.

Seen this way, oscillation in intimacy is not confusion or immaturity. It is responsiveness to conditions that do not always hold both union and separation well.

Noticing this doesn’t settle the movement. It doesn’t decide how close to be.

It simply places the experience within the realities that shape intimacy, where coming together and pulling back are often part of learning how to stay connected without disappearing.
