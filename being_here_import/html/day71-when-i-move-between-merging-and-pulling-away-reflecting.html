When I move between merging and pulling away — Reflecting

When I move between merging and pulling away — LUST, CARE, and PLAY cycle between merging and pulling away. This sits within Complex 8 (Intimacy & Fusion), in its oscillating state. This stays close to inner experience, naming the push–pull of intimacy without trying to settle it, interpret it, or guide it.

There are moments when closeness feels inviting. When sharing feels natural. When being together brings ease.

And then, sometimes quite suddenly, that same closeness can feel like too much. Space becomes necessary. Pulling back feels like relief.

The movement between these states can be hard to track. One day, connection feels right. The next, it feels heavy or confusing.

From the inside, this can raise questions. Why do I want closeness and then resist it? Why does something that felt good start to feel uncomfortable?

Often, both movements carry meaning. Merging can bring warmth, belonging, aliveness. Pulling away can restore clarity, autonomy, and breath.

Neither response cancels the other. They arise in relation to capacity, to history, to how safe or saturated the moment feels.

The mind may look for a stable preference. A single answer. A way of being that doesn’t change.

But intimacy rarely moves in straight lines. It unfolds through approach and retreat, through opening and pausing, through learning where edges live.

Recognising this doesn’t smooth the rhythm. It doesn’t resolve the tension.

It simply allows the experience of moving between merging and pulling away to be seen honestly, as part of how closeness is negotiated without losing oneself.
