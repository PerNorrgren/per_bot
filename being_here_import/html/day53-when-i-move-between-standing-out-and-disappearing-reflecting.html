When I move between standing out and disappearing — Reflecting

When I move between standing out and disappearing — RAGE, FEAR, and SEEKING cycle between visibility and withdrawal. This sits within Complex 6 (Status & Hierarchy), in its oscillating state. This stays close to inner experience, naming fluctuation around visibility without resolving it or asking it to stabilise.

There are moments when being seen feels right. When speaking up feels natural. When presence carries energy rather than risk.

And then, just as clearly, there are moments when pulling back feels safer. When quiet feels protective. When being less visible brings relief.

The movement between these states can be subtle. A shift in who is present. A change in tone. A sense that the ground has altered.

From the inside, this can feel inconsistent. Why do I show up fully here and disappear there? Why does confidence come and go?

Often, both responses are sensible. Visibility carries opportunity. Withdrawal reduces exposure. Each serves a different need.

The body may respond before the mind catches up. Leaning forward when it feels supported. Stepping back when the cost feels higher.

It’s easy to judge this movement. To wish for steadiness. To want a single, reliable way of being.

But lived presence is rarely fixed. It adjusts to context, to power, to how welcome visibility feels.

Recognising this doesn’t stop the shifting. It doesn’t lock presence into one form.

It simply allows the experience of moving between standing out and disappearing to be seen with more understanding, as a responsive pattern rather than a personal flaw.
