When I can’t stop pushing forward — Meeting It Head On

When I can’t stop pushing forward — SEEKING and RAGE run high. This sits within Complex 3 (Agency & Reward), in its over-activated state. This poem implicitly carries all nine somatic / salience steps in order, and works with excess agency — momentum without rest, effort without arrival — without dampening vitality or ambition. It allows doing to settle into being, without collapse or loss of direction.

There is room here to pause without falling behind.

Nothing important is lost by being still for a moment.

The body is already supported,

held by something solid beneath it,

not needing to brace or lean.

Awareness settles into where you are now,

not scanning for the next step,

not calculating what comes after.

Muscles release their constant readiness,

not giving up strength,

just resting it where it belongs.

Breath moves without instruction,

no longer pulled upward by urgency,

no longer driven by effort.

Warmth gathers steadily,

not sharp,

not explosive,

contained and usable.

The chest softens without collapsing.

Energy doesn’t disappear —

it reorganises.

The urge to act is allowed to quiet

without being silenced.

Direction remains,

but it no longer needs to hurry.

The body recognises that movement

is more effective when it can stop and start,

rather than push endlessly.

There is a sense of standing inside yourself,

not ahead of yourself,

able to feel what is present before deciding what comes next.

This moment does not ask you to quit.

It does not ask you to slow down forever.

It simply allows you to be here,

with your energy gathered,

your agency intact,

and your next step not yet required.

For now, that is enough.
