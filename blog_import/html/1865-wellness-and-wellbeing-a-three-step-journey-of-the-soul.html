Wellness and Wellbeing - Gratitude for Personal Growth

In pursuit of Wellness and Wellbeing, we often overlook the most essential companion—ourselves. In the hustle and bustle of daily routines, our achievements, no matter how minor, tend to go unnoticed. Yet, acknowledging these small victories is crucial for our mental and emotional well-being. One profound practice that can foster this acknowledgement is expressing gratitude to oneself. This practice, infused with kindness and a touch of humour, can significantly enhance our self-care routine. Drawing inspiration from the Dalai Lama’s teachings, let’s explore how to embrace self-gratitude through visualisation, affirmation, and compassionate gestures.

Visualisation

Visualisation is a powerful tool to reconnect with our daily actions and achievements. It can be essential for Wellness and Wellbeing. By closing our eyes and reflecting on the day, we can bring to mind three things we did for ourselves. These actions need not be grand; they could be as simple as making a healthy meal, taking a necessary break, or completing a small but significant task.

Imagine a day where you decided to prepare a nourishing breakfast. As you recall the effort of choosing fresh ingredients and cooking with care, you realise that this simple act was a gift to your body and mind. Similarly, taking a break when feeling overwhelmed is a gesture of kindness towards your mental health, allowing you to recharge and continue with renewed energy. Completing a task, no matter how small, is a testament to your dedication and capability.

Affirmation

Affirmation is the next step in this self-gratitude practice. As you visualise each action, acknowledge it with a heartfelt “thank you.” For instance, say to yourself, “Thank you for making a healthy meal. This shows that I care about my wellness and I appreciate myself.” These affirmations reinforce the idea that you are worthy of love and care, not just from others but from yourself.

One personal anecdote that highlights the power of affirmation involves a close friend who, after years of neglecting his health, started acknowledging his efforts to live a healthier lifestyle. Each time he chose a salad over fast food, he would say, “Thank you for choosing what’s best for me.” Over time, this simple affirmation boosted his confidence and commitment to his health journey.

Compassionate Gesture

The final step is to pair your visualisation and affirmation with a compassionate gesture. Imagine giving yourself a warm smile or a pat on the back. This might seem trivial, but these gestures are powerful symbols of self-acceptance and recognition. They serve as physical manifestations of your inner kindness.

Consider a story shared by the Dalai Lama about a young monk who struggled with self-doubt. The monk was advised to smile at himself each morning in the mirror. Initially, it felt awkward, but as days passed, this compassionate gesture became a source of joy and confidence for him. Smiling at oneself can similarly transform our perception, making us more aware of our worth and accomplishments.

Everyday Acts of Self-Gratitude

Incorporating self-gratitude into daily life can be transformative. Here are some real-life examples:

1.  The Busy Professional: Jane, a marketing executive, often found herself overwhelmed by deadlines. She started a habit of ending her day by jotting down three things she did well, like managing a difficult client or finishing a report ahead of schedule. Each note was followed by a self-affirmation and a smile. This practice not only reduced her stress but also boosted her confidence.

2.  The New Parent: Michael, a new father, was exhausted from sleepless nights and constant baby care. He began to appreciate his efforts by saying, “Thank you for being patient with the baby today.” He would then give himself a gentle pat on the shoulder. This small act of self-gratitude helped him stay positive and motivated.

3.  The Student: Emma, a university student, struggled with balancing studies and part-time work. She started recognising her efforts by visualising her accomplishments, like finishing an assignment or attending a tough lecture, and saying, “Thank you for your hard work.” She would then smile at herself in the mirror. This routine provided her with a sense of achievement and balance.

Conclusion: The Path to Personal Well-Being

Expressing gratitude to oneself is a practice rooted in kindness, much like the teachings of the Dalai Lama. It encourages us to celebrate our daily efforts and recognise our self-worth. By visualising our achievements, affirming our actions, and offering compassionate gestures, we nurture a loving relationship with ourselves.

In a world where we are often our harshest critics, let us choose to be our own best friends. As you embrace this practice, remember to infuse it with humour and warmth. After all, as the Dalai Lama often reminds us, true happiness comes from a heart that knows kindness—especially towards oneself. So, take a moment each day to thank yourself, smile, and acknowledge that you are deserving of love, connection and appreciation.
