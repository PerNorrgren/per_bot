Mastering Stoic Wisdom: Control and Acceptance in Stoicism and Mindfulness

Originally published 2024-06-22

Greetings,
In this blog I delve into one of the foundational maxims of Stoic philosophy: “Control what you can; accept what you cannot.” This principle, though simple in its articulation, is profound in its application, offering a path to tranquillity and resilience in the face of life’s inevitable challenges. In its core it resonates deeply with learning Mindfulness and practicing mindfulness.

Understanding the Maxim

At its core, this maxim instructs us to distinguish between what lies within our power and what does not. In every situation, we must ask ourselves: “Is this something I can change?” If the answer is yes, we should take deliberate action. If no, we must learn to accept it with equanimity.

This discernment is crucial. Much of human distress arises from our futile attempts to control the uncontrollable. We waste energy and peace of mind on matters beyond our influence, such as other people’s opinions, the past, or the inevitable march of time.

Practical Application of the Maxim

One way to embody this principle, could be this: begin by categorising events and circumstances in your life into two distinct groups:

1.  Things Within Your Control:
•   Your thoughts
•   Your actions
•   Your reactions
•   Your efforts and decisions
2.  Things Beyond Your Control:
•   Other people’s behaviour and thoughts
•   Past events
•   Natural occurrences
•   Outcomes and results

By focusing your efforts solely on what you can control, you conserve your energy for meaningful action. Simultaneously, accepting what you cannot control frees you from unnecessary stress and anxiety.

Applying the Maxim in Daily Life

Consider a scenario where you face criticism at work. If the critique is within your control—perhaps rooted in your performance—take constructive steps to improve. However, if the criticism stems from another’s personal bias, accept it as their opinion and move on, knowing you cannot alter their perception.

Cultivating Acceptance Through Mindfulness

The practice of mindfulness is instrumental in mastering this maxim. Mindfulness trains us to observe our thoughts and feelings without immediate reaction, creating a space where we can discern control from acceptance. This heightened awareness allows us to respond thoughtfully rather than react impulsively.
